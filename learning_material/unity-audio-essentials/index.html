Learning outcomes

Build a soundscape whose behavior can be explained and tested.

The finished kitchen combines a stable music layer with continuous and intermittent environmental sounds. Each audible result should trace back to an asset, a component, a setting, or the listener’s position.

  • Distinguish an Audio Clip, Audio Source, and Audio Listener.
  • Configure continuous and triggered playback behavior.
  • Choose 2D or 3D audio from the sound’s role in the scene.
  • Verify distance and direction with controlled listener movement.
  • Layer and balance music, appliance sound, and exterior ambience.
  • Diagnose common silent, constant-volume, and mistimed results.
Opening demonstration Run a finished kitchen once. First stand still and identify each layer. Then walk toward the pot, rotate left and right, and cross the room. Ask which sounds changed, which stayed constant, and which component explains each result.
01 · SOURCE → LISTENER MODEL

Start from silence and map the audio system

Unity separates audio data from playback behavior. An Audio Clip stores sound data; an Audio Source plays an assigned generator from a GameObject; one active Audio Listener receives the scene’s audible sources.

  1. In the Project window, open _Unity Essentials → Scenes → 3_Kitchen_Audio_Scene.
  2. Enter Play mode and explore the silent kitchen with WASD and the mouse.
  3. Exit Play mode. In the Hierarchy window, select Main Camera.
  4. In the Inspector window, locate its Audio Listener component.
  5. Confirm that the scene has exactly one active Audio Listener.
ElementWhere it livesResponsibility
Audio ClipProject windowStores imported sound data as a reusable asset.
Audio GeneratorAudio Source InspectorReferences the Audio Clip or Audio Random Container to play.
Audio SourceGameObject componentControls playback, volume, looping, and spatial behavior.
Audio ListenerUsually Main CameraReceives audible sources from the player’s position and orientation.
One listener Keep one active Audio Listener in the scene. Two listeners do not represent left and right ears; multiple active listeners produce an Editor warning and an ambiguous mix.

02 · CONTINUOUS ENVIRONMENTAL SOUND

Attach a looping source to the boiling pot

A sound that belongs to a visible object should originate from that object. Add the pot first, then place the Audio Source component on the same GameObject so its Transform defines the sound’s world position.

  1. From _Unity Essentials → Prefabs → Kitchen, drag Boiling_Pot onto a stove burner.
  2. Select Boiling_Pot, choose Add Component, and add Audio Source.
  3. In the Audio Source component, use the object picker for Audio Generator.
  4. Assign Bubbling_Water.
  5. Enable Loop so the continuous appliance sound restarts at the end of the clip.
  6. Leave Spatial Blend at 0 temporarily, then enter Play mode.
  7. Walk near and far from the pot. The clip should play, but its presence should remain constant for this first test.
Version label Unity 6.3 names the assignment field Audio Generator. Some embedded Unity Learn videos were recorded in earlier Unity 6 builds and display Audio Resource or AudioClip in the same position. Follow the current 6.3 label.
Controlled observation With Spatial Blend still at 0, compare the pot from beside the stove and across the room. Record the unexpected result: the object moved relative to the listener, but the sound did not. This establishes the failure that 3D audio will correct.

03 · 2D VERSUS 3D AUDIO

Make position and orientation audible

Spatial Blend controls how strongly Unity’s 3D audio calculations affect a source. At 0, playback ignores world position. At 1, distance and direction between the source and listener affect the result.

2D · SPATIAL BLEND 0

Position-independent. Use it for nondiegetic music or interface audio that should remain stable as the listener moves.

3D · SPATIAL BLEND 1

Position-dependent. Use it for visible or local environmental sources such as a pot, refrigerator, or bird.

  1. Select Boiling_Pot and set its Audio Source Spatial Blend to 1.
  2. Enter Play mode and stop across the room from the pot.
  3. Walk directly toward the stove. The boiling should become more present as distance decreases.
  4. Stand beside the pot and rotate left, right, and away. Listen for the source direction to change with listener orientation.
  5. Exit Play mode before adjusting settings.
PropertyWhat it controlsTest
Spatial BlendHow much 3D processing affects the sourceCompare position-independent 0 with fully 3D 1.
Min DistanceInner region where volume remains at its maximumWalk just inside and outside the boundary.
Max DistanceOuter distance behavior, dependent on rolloff modeDo not assume silence; inspect Volume Rolloff.
Volume RolloffHow volume changes as distance increasesCompare the same route after changing only this mode.
A/B spatial test Test the same near–far–rotate route at Spatial Blend 0 and 1. Change only Spatial Blend. The second run should add distance and directional evidence without changing the clip or Transform.

04 · POSITION-INDEPENDENT LAYER

Add background music without placing it in the room

The boiling pot is diegetic: it has a visible source inside the world. Background music is nondiegetic in this scene: it supports the overall atmosphere and should not become louder near one wall or quieter near another.

  1. Right-click empty space in the Hierarchy window and choose Audio → Audio Source.
  2. Rename the new GameObject Background Music.
  3. In the Project window, open _Unity Essentials → Audio → Music.
  4. Select candidate clips and use the Preview panel at the bottom of the Inspector window.
  5. Assign one clip to the Background Music Audio Source’s Audio Generator.
  6. Enable Loop, set Volume between approximately 0.1 and 0.5, and keep Spatial Blend at 0.
  7. Enter Play mode, walk through the kitchen, and balance the music against the boiling pot.
Mix check Stand at the doorway, center, and stove. The music should remain present at all three positions, while the pot should change. Reduce music Volume if it masks the quieter far-distance pot test.
Save outside Play mode Exit Play mode before making final changes, then save with Ctrl/Cmd + S. Changes made during Play mode are temporary.

05 · INTERMITTENT EXTERIOR SOUND

Trigger localized bird calls at varied intervals

A continuous loop would make birds sound mechanical. Place separate 3D sources outside, disable automatic startup, and let the provided interval component trigger each source at different times.

ControlBehaviorUse here
Play On AwakeStarts playback when the component becomes activeDisable for birds.
LoopRestarts the same clip at its endLeave disabled for individual chirps.
Play Sound at Random IntervalsTriggers playback between minimum and maximum delaysAdd to every bird source.
  1. Create another Audio Source and move it outside a window or near an implied tree.
  2. Rename it Bird_1 and assign SFX_Bird_1 to Audio Generator.
  3. Set Spatial Blend to 1.
  4. Disable Play On Awake.
  5. Choose Add Component and add Play Sound at Random Intervals.
  6. Duplicate the source as Bird_2 and Bird_3. Give them different clips and exterior positions.
  7. Enter Play mode and listen long enough to hear more than one interval from more than one position.
Timing and location test Start Play mode and wait without moving. The birds should not all fire at scene load. Then face each exterior source and turn away; calls should arrive at varied times and from distinct directions.

06 · MIXING + FAILURE DIAGNOSIS

Change one layer at a time and diagnose the result

A layered mix is easier to reason about when each source is tested independently before the full scene. Mute or disable one layer, repeat the same listener route, and change one property between runs.

  1. Test the pot alone at far, middle, and near positions.
  2. Add music and repeat the route. Reduce music Volume until the pot’s distance change remains audible.
  3. Add birds and wait through several trigger intervals.
  4. Inspect the Hierarchy: source names should reveal their roles without selecting them.
  5. Exit Play mode, apply the final values, and save the scene.
Observed resultLikely causeInspect
A source is silentNo generator, muted/disabled source, or no triggerAudio Generator, Mute, Play On Awake, interval component
Pot never changes with movementSpatial Blend is still 0Boiling_Pot Audio Source

Bird plays at scene startPlay On Awake is enabledBird Audio Source
Bird never repeatsInterval component is absent or disabledPlay Sound at Random Intervals
Editor reports listener conflictMore than one active Audio ListenerCamera and player GameObjects
Evidence before adjustment When a result is wrong, state what you heard, the one setting you predict is responsible, and the next controlled test. Change the setting only after making the prediction.
07 · CORE APPLICATION TASK · REQUIRED

Build and prove a three-layer kitchen soundscape

Produce one saved scene that combines the lecture’s central systems. This is the required lecture task.

LayerRequired configurationObservable evidence
Boiling potLooping Bubbling_Water; Spatial Blend 1Louder near the stove and direction changes when turning.
Background musicLooping music; reduced Volume; Spatial Blend 0Presence stays consistent at three listener positions.
Exterior birdsAt least two 3D sources; Play On Awake off; interval componentCalls occur at varied times and from distinct locations.
  1. Confirm exactly one active Audio Listener on the Main Camera.
  2. Start at the doorway and listen for at least ten seconds without moving.
  3. Walk to the stove and rotate left, right, and away from the pot.
  4. Cross the kitchen and verify that music remains stable while the pot changes.
  5. Wait for at least two bird calls and identify their different directions.
  6. Exit Play mode, save the scene, and capture either one Hierarchy/Inspector screenshot or a short written test log.
Completion evidence The task is complete when one saved scene demonstrates all three behaviors and your screenshot or test log identifies the setting responsible for each behavior. A scene that merely contains the clips is not yet complete.
08 · OPTIONAL EXTENSION TASK

Optional: design an acoustic boundary with reverb

This task is optional and does not affect lecture completion. It extends source–listener distance into room acoustics: the listener’s position inside a zone changes how audible sources reverberate.

  1. Right-click the Hierarchy window and choose Audio → Audio Reverb Zone.
  2. Use the Move tool to place the zone at the center of the kitchen.
  3. Inspect the inner Min Distance and outer Max Distance gizmos.
  4. Enter Play mode and compare Room with one exaggerated preset such as Cave or Underwater.
  5. Walk from outside the zone through its boundary and into the center.
  6. Choose a final preset that fits the kitchen, exit Play mode, and save it.
  7. Write one sentence explaining where the effect begins, where it becomes strongest, and why the final preset fits.
Extension evidence Your comparison should name both presets and describe a position-dependent change. “It sounded different” is insufficient; identify where the transition began and which preset you kept.
